How it works

When armed, every spin on a wheelofnames.com tab glides to the chosen name. The wheel still spins and decelerates normally, so it looks real. Flip the switch off for a fair spin. If the name isn't on the wheel, it spins fairly (so you won't get caught by an obvious mismatch).
